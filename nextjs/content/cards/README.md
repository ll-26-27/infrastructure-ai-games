Each card is one thinker and one idea, with a quotation and its source, and a **turn**: a question that puts the idea to work on a game or an infrastructure.

On their own, these are flash cards. You could learn them all and be able to say what each thinker thought, and nothing would change in how you see a game or a bridge. The cards start to work when they are linked: each one names the nodes it is about in the [course graph](/graphs/course) or the [games graph](/graphs/games), and shows up there joined to them. A card about one side can become the bridge to the other.

Three cards have no quotation yet. They are for your own reading: copy in the sentence that matters, with its page.

**Print them** on 11 x 17 at [/graphs/cards/print](/graphs/cards/print), cut them apart, and lay them out on a table beside the games.

**Add your own.** Copy `nextjs/content/cards/_template.md`, give it a new name, and fill it in. The cards you made on September 28 belong here too.
