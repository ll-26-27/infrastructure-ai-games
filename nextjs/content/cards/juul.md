> To play a video game is therefore to interact with real rules while imagining a fictional world, and a video game is a set of rules as well as a fictional world.

Jesper Juul, *Half-Real: Video Games between Real Rules and Fictional Worlds* (MIT Press, 2005), p. 1.

**Turn it.** The fiction can be anything; the rules really run. In a game about AI, which part is fiction and which part is real?
