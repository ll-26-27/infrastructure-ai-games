> To play a video game is therefore to interact with real rules while imagining a fictional world, and a video game is a set of rules as well as a fictional world.

Jesper Juul, *Half-Real: Video Games between Real Rules and Fictional Worlds* (MIT Press, 2005), p. 1.

Juul argues that video games are half real: the rules are real, actually enforced, and you really win or lose, while the world they describe is fiction. Playing means dealing with real rules while imagining a fictional world, and both halves shape the experience.
