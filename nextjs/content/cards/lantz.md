> I thought, in a game like this, where the whole point is that you're in pursuit of maximizing a particular arbitrary quantity, it would be so funny if you were an AI and making paperclips. That game would design itself, I thought.

Frank Lantz, quoted in Adam Rogers, *Wired*, October 21, 2017.

**Turn it.** Most clicker games already have an arbitrary goal. What did making the player an AI change?
