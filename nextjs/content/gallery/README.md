Grow from almost nothing, turn one resource into another, put people to work, spread until the whole map is yours: these games do something close to what you are going to build. Some you may have played for a hundred hours. Most you have probably never heard of. Look for the same few mechanics turning up again and again, in a 1983 game about farming on another planet and in a pizza shop you ran in a school computer lab.

Each game has a page with when it was made, who made it, what you do in it, which [mechanics](/mechanics) it uses, and where to play it. To add a game you know well, copy `nextjs/content/gallery/_template.md`, rename it, and fill it in.
