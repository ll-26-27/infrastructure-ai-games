The screen says "the room is cold" and gives you one button: light fire. Keep it going and a stranger arrives. Then you gather wood, build huts and traps, and as villagers come you give them trades: gatherer, hunter, trapper, tanner and more, each turning one resource into another. Later you leave the village to explore the world outside, and find out what happened to it.

It is made almost entirely of text and buttons, and its code is open on GitHub.

**Look for:** how much a few words do. With no pictures at all, a list of jobs and a line of text is enough to make a world you want to keep growing.
