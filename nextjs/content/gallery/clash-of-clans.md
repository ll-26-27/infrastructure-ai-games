Your village has mines and collectors that fill with gold and elixir over time. You spend them on walls, defenses, army camps and upgrades, each of which takes real hours or days to finish. Then you train an army and attack other players' villages for their resources, while they attack yours.

It is one of the most-played games of the last decade, and most people who play it would not call it a strategy game. It has the 4X skeleton anyway: expand, exploit what you hold, and take from others.

**Look for:** the timer. The thing you are really managing is time, and the game sells you more of it.
