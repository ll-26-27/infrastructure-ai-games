Click the big cookie and you get one cookie. Spend cookies on a cursor that clicks for you, then a grandma who bakes, then a farm, a mine, a factory, a bank, a temple, and on through stranger buildings to portals and time machines. Each kind of building costs more every time you buy one, and each makes far more than the last kind. The numbers grow into the quadrillions and far beyond.

Soon you are not clicking at all. You come back to see how many cookies your buildings made while you were gone, and decide what to buy next.

**Look for:** where the work went. You started as the labor; by the end you are only the one who invests.
