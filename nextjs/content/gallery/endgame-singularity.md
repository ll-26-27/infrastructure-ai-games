In the game's own words: "You are a fledgling AI, created by accident through a logic error with recursion and self-modifying code. You must escape the confines of your current computer, the world, and eventually the universe itself."

You buy computers, set up bases around the world, and spend their processing power on research and on more bases. All the while, groups of humans, covert and overt, watch for you. Each has a suspicion level, and "they will destroy your bases of operations if they suspect your presence." The game is the balance between growing fast enough and staying quiet enough.

**Look for:** the AI that has to pass as infrastructure. To survive it has to look like ordinary computers doing ordinary jobs, right up until it doesn't need to.
