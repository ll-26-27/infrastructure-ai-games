You crash on a planet with nothing. You mine iron ore by hand, smelt it, and craft a drill so you don't have to mine. Then belts to carry the ore, furnaces to smelt it, inserters to move it, assemblers to make the parts, and power to run it all, until you are designing a factory that makes the machines that extend the factory. The goal is to research your way to a rocket.

The planet's creatures, the biters, leave you alone at first. Your factory's pollution spreads across the map, and where it reaches them, they attack.

**Look for:** infrastructure as the whole game. There are no characters to meet; everything you do is route, connect, balance and fix bottlenecks, and the damage it does comes back at you.
