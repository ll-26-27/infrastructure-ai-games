You plow, plant and harvest on a small farm and sell the crops for coins to buy more land, buildings and decorations. Crops take real time to grow, and if you don't come back in time they wither. Your Facebook friends were your neighbors: they could help on your farm and send gifts, and the game kept asking you to ask them.

At its peak it was one of the most-played games in the world. It closed when Facebook stopped supporting Flash at the end of 2020.

**Look for:** your friends as a resource. The game's growth came from turning your social network into part of the farm.
