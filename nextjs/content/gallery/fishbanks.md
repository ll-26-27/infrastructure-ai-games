Teams each run a fishing company. Every round you buy, sell or build ships, and choose whether to send them to the coastal waters or the deep sea. The fish reproduce, but only so fast, and the number left depends on what everyone caught. Each team is trying to finish with the most money.

It was made to teach how a shared resource collapses even when no one wants it to: the "tragedy of the commons." The instructor can add auctions, permits and quotas to see what changes. (The year it was first used is uncertain; it was in classrooms by the late 1980s.)

**Look for:** the fish you can't see. Nobody knows exactly how many are left until it is too late, and that missing number is the whole game.
