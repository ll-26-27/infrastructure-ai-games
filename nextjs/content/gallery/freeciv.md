Three university students in Denmark started Freeciv in 1996 as a free game in the style of Civilization, and volunteers have kept it going for thirty years. Cities work the tiles around them, settlers found new cities, technologies unlock one another, and the game runs from early settlements to the space race.

Because its code is open, anyone can read how it works: how a city decides what it produces, how the computer players choose. Some games on its browser server run for weeks, with a dozen or more players taking their turns online.

**Look for:** who keeps it running. A game nobody owns still needs servers, maintainers and rules about who decides; the infrastructure here is the volunteers.
