The world has frozen. Your city is a ring of tents around one coal-burning generator, and everything depends on keeping it lit. You send people to gather coal, wood, steel and food, build shelters and workshops in rings around the heat, and decide who works which job.

When there isn't enough to go around, you pass laws: longer shifts, soup stretched with sawdust, children sent to work. Each law keeps people alive and changes what kind of city it is. Two meters, Hope and Discontent, tell you how the people feel about it, and if either fails, you lose.

**Look for:** the law as a building. Here a policy is a thing you place, like a workshop, and it shapes the city the same way.
