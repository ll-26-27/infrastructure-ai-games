Buy seeds at a shop that restocks every few minutes, plant them, harvest the crops and sell them for Sheckles, the game's money, then buy more seeds, tools and pets. Its own description promises that "your garden literally grows while you are offline." Other players' gardens are right next to yours, and you can trade with them, or steal from them.

It launched in March 2025, and by the summer more than 21 million people were playing it at the same time, more than any game on Roblox before it.

**Look for:** the oldest farming loop in this gallery (plant, wait, harvest, sell, expand) inside one of the newest games, and how little it had to change.
