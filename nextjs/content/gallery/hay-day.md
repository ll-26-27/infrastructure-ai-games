You grow wheat and corn, raise chickens and cows, and send what they produce through machines: a bakery, a dairy, a feed mill. Wheat becomes bread, milk becomes cheese, corn becomes feed for the chickens that lay the eggs for the cake. A truck and a boat bring orders, and filling them earns the coins and experience to unlock more machines. Other players buy and sell at your roadside shop.

**Look for:** the production chain. Every order is a short list, and behind each item is a chain of fields, animals, machines and waiting.
