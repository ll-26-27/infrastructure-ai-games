It starts with you gathering catnip by hand. Build a catnip field, and a hut, and kittens move in. Now you decide what each kitten does: woodcutter, farmer, scholar, hunter, miner, priest, and later geologist, engineer and ambassador. Every resource feeds another, research unlocks new buildings, and the village grows, slowly, toward the stars.

Almost all of it is text and numbers. It is labor allocation, the core of a 4X game, with the map taken away.

**Look for:** the job screen. The whole economy is a list of who does what, and moving one kitten from farmer to scholar is the only decision that matters.
