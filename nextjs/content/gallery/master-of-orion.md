A turn-based space strategy game: you lead one species from its home planet out into a galaxy of unexplored stars, colonize the planets worth having, research new technology, and fight or negotiate with the other species doing the same.

Before it came out, a magazine writer previewing it gave it a rating of "XXXX" because it had "the essential four X's of any good strategic conquest game": explore, expand, exploit and exterminate. The name stuck for the whole kind of game.

**Look for:** the four verbs. Try sorting every action in any game you know into explore, expand, exploit or exterminate, and see what doesn't fit.
