Four colonists land on the planet Irata. Each turn you claim a plot of land, buy a M.U.L.E. (a robot worker), outfit it to farm food, make energy or mine ore, and set it to work. Then everyone meets at the auction, where buyers and sellers walk toward each other on screen to set a price, and shortages push prices up.

Each player is trying to get richest, but if the colony as a whole runs out of food or energy, everyone suffers. It is one of the first games about an economy, and one of the first designed for four people playing at once.

**Look for:** the market as a place. Prices here are not numbers in a menu; they are what happens when four people decide to sell or hold.
