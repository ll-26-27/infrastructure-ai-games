Four diseases are spreading across a map of 48 cities, shown as colored cubes. Each player has a role (medic, scientist, dispatcher, researcher and others) with its own special power, and together you travel, treat cities and collect sets of cards to discover cures. You all win or all lose.

After each turn new cities get infected. When a city with three cubes gets another, it has an outbreak and spreads to every city connected to it, which can set off more outbreaks in a chain. Every so often an Epidemic card makes everything worse at once.

**Look for:** the outbreak chain. One city too many and the problem jumps along every connection; this is how a breakdown in one part of an infrastructure spreads to the rest.
