You are running Papa Louie's pizza shop. Customers line up and order their pizzas exactly: these toppings, here, baked this long, cut into so many slices. You take the order, build the pizza, bake it and cut it, moving between stations while more customers arrive. The closer each pizza is to the order and the faster it comes, the more you earn.

It was the first of the Papa's restaurant games (burgers, tacos, pancakes, and many more). The later games let you spend your tips on upgrades for the shop.

**Look for:** the ticket. Every customer becomes a list of exact specifications, and the job is to turn the list into a thing as fast as possible.
