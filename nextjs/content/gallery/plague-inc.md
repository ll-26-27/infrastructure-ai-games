You start with one infected person, Patient Zero, in a country you choose. As the disease spreads you earn points to spend on its evolution: how it travels (by air, water, livestock), what symptoms it causes, how it resists drugs and cold. Travel routes carry it across borders, and countries close airports and ports as they notice. Meanwhile the world's scientists work on a cure, shown as a bar that keeps filling.

The balance is the game: too deadly too early and the world notices and moves to stop you; too slow and the cure arrives first.

**Look for:** the world as a network. The map is airports, ports and borders, and the infrastructure that connects people is the thing the disease rides.
