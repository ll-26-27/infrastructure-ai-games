Your tribe starts with one city on a small square map covered in clouds. Each turn your cities make stars, the game's one currency, and you spend them on technologies, units and city upgrades. You grow by capturing villages and other tribes' cities.

In its Perfection mode, you have thirty turns to score as many points as you can; in Domination, you play until one tribe is left. A whole game of Civilization's kind of thinking can fit into a bus ride.
