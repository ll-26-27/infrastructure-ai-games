From the makers of Plague Inc., the opposite job. The war is over, and you are trying to bring a region (inspired by Afghanistan) under a stable government. Each month you have a budget to spend, zone by zone, on civilian projects like healthcare, education, water, roads, electricity and phones; on the government; and on troops.

Each zone has a level of **Support** for the government, and you have to guard your **Reputation**. Spending fast raises corruption and inflation. An insurgency spreads across the map; you can fight it or negotiate with it. You win by stabilizing every zone.

**Look for:** infrastructure as the way you win people over. Here roads and healthcare are not scenery; they are what support is made of, and the game counts what goes wrong when they are built too fast.
