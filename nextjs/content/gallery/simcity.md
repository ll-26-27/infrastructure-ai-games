You never build a house. You mark land as residential, commercial or industrial, connect it with power lines, roads and rail, and set the tax rate. Then the simulated residents decide whether to build there, depending on power, traffic, pollution, crime and what is nearby. The city is something you lay down infrastructure for, not something you make.

In 2008 the original game's code was released as free software under the name Micropolis, so you can play it today in a browser.

**Look for:** the zoning map as a database. You only ever choose a category for each square; everything you see grows out of those categories and the lines that connect them.
