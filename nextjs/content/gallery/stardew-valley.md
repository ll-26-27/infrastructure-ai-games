You leave an office job for your late grandfather's overgrown farm near Pelican Town. You farm, fish, forage and mine, and turn what you gather into goods to sell. The town's Community Center is falling apart; you can restore it by filling bundles of crops, fish and goods, room by room.

Or you can buy a membership at JojaMart, the big store in town. The Community Center becomes a Joja warehouse, and the same repairs are bought with money instead of made from what you grew.

**Look for:** two ways to build the same infrastructure. The bridge gets fixed either way; the game asks you what it means that you paid for it instead.
