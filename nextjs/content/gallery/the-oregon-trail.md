It is 1848 and you are taking a wagon from Missouri to Oregon. You start in a store with a fixed amount of money and buy oxen, food, clothing, ammunition and spare parts. On the trail you set the pace and the rations, decide when to rest and when to hunt, and choose at each river whether to ford it, float the wagon or pay for a ferry. Every choice changes the odds of a broken wheel, a sickness or a death.

Three student teachers wrote the first version in 1971 for a history class in Minneapolis. The 1985 version was in American school computer labs for decades.

**Look for:** what the store teaches. The hardest decisions happen before you leave: everything after that is living with what you bought.
