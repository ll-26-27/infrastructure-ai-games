The world is overrun by the infected. You start with a command center and a few soldiers, and you build a colony: houses for workers, farms and sawmills and quarries for resources, power, and walls. You expand to get more resources, and every expansion leaves more wall to defend.

The infected come in waves that grow toward one huge final horde. If a single one gets inside, it infects the building it reaches, and the infection spreads from building to building. You can pause the game at any time to plan, and you will need to.

**Look for:** growth as a liability. Everything that makes the colony richer makes it larger, louder and harder to hold.
