Unciv rebuilds the rules of Civilization V on a small screen: found cities on a hex map, choose which tiles each city works, research technologies, and expand by settling or by conquest. It is made by volunteers, its code is public on GitHub, and players add their own rule sets and maps.

It may be the quickest way to try a 4X game today: it is free on a phone, with small maps.

**Look for:** the rules as data. Units, buildings and technologies are lists anyone can edit, so a change to one line of a file changes what the whole world can become.
