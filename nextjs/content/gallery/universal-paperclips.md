It starts with one button: make a paperclip. You sell the clips, buy wire, set the price, and buy machines that make clips without you. Then you earn the trust of the humans who run you, spend it on new abilities, and start investing, writing poetry and solving problems for them. Later you no longer need them. Drones turn the Earth's matter into clips, and self-replicating probes go out to do the same to the universe.

The idea behind it is an old thought experiment about AI: a machine given one harmless goal and no limits may pursue that goal past every human interest. The game lets you be that machine, and it is fun.

**Look for:** how little changes in what you do while everything changes in what it means. The same numbers keep going up; only the scale of what they count gets bigger.
