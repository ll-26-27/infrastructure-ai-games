UFOs are appearing around the world. You run X-COM, an organization funded by sixteen countries. On the globe you build bases, track and shoot down UFOs, and research the alien technology you recover. On the ground your soldiers fight turn by turn, and when a soldier dies, they are gone for good.

At the end of each month the countries look at how you did in their region and raise or cut your funding. Aliens can infiltrate a country's government, and that country leaves the project. Two bad months and you are finished.

**Look for:** the funders. The war is fought on two maps: the battlefield, and the monthly report to the governments that decide whether the war continues.
