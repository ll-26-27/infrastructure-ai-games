Pick an ancient civilization (Athenians, Spartans, Romans, Persians, Carthaginians and others) and build a city in real time. Your citizen-soldiers gather food, wood, stone and metal, build houses and barracks, and pick up spears when the city is attacked. You decide how many work the fields and how many go to war.

It began in 2001 as an idea for changing Age of Empires II, became its own game, and released its code in 2009. Its first public version came out in 2010, and volunteers are still building it.

**Look for:** the citizen-soldier. The same person farms and fights, so every soldier you send away is food you didn't gather.
