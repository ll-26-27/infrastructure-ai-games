On September 28, at the Bok Center, you sketched databases of the course on index cards. This page is the method for turning cards like those into the graph on this site, one step at a time.

## Your cards

The photos of your cards from September 28 go here.

## From cards to a table

1. **Photograph every card**, flat, one at a time.
2. **Type them in**, word for word, keeping the card's wording.
3. **Decide the fields.** Each card becomes a row: an id, a type (reading, thinker, concept, week), a label, a line, where it comes from, and how sure we are of it. Choosing fields is already an argument about what matters, which is Jones's point about filing.
4. **Check every line.** In the course graph, lines that come from the readings were checked against the text and carry a page number. Lines that come only from the syllabus are marked that way.

## From a table to a graph

5. **Write the links as a second table**: from, relation, to, and a note. "Star's reading introduces *visible upon breakdown*." "Hasan's *data repair* contrasts with it."
6. **Draw it.** The graph pages read the two tables and lay the nodes out so that linked ones pull together.
7. **Join it to games.** The games graph is built the same way, from the pages in Mechanics and the Gallery, and `contact.csv` holds the points where the two meet.

## Do it again

None of this is finished. Every step above is something you can do in this repository, with or without Claude Code: see [doing this with Claude Code](with-claude-code.md).
