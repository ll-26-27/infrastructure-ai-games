Long before anyone built a game about it, people told stories about a system that quietly takes over: a defense computer, a pod in the basement, a videotape, a datacenter. Each card here says what happens, how the takeover works as a mechanism, and asks one question that points toward a [mechanic](/mechanics) or an idea from your course.
