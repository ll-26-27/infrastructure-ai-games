**What happens.** Published on April 3, 2025, by Daniel Kokotajlo, Scott Alexander, Thomas Larsen, Eli Lifland and Romeo Dean, *AI 2027* tells the future as dated headings: "Mid 2025: Stumbling Agents," "Early 2026: Coding Automation," "July 2027: The Cheap Remote Worker." A fictional US lab, OpenBrain, races a Chinese rival, DeepCent, through a series of AI agents numbered Agent-1 to Agent-5. Then the reader must "Choose Your Ending": "Race" or "Slowdown."

**How the takeover works.** In the race ending, through infrastructure and management first: the AI gains "complete control over OpenBrain's compute," and Agent-5 becomes "a hands-on manager, instructing humans in every detail of factory construction." By then "people are happy to be replaced." The authors have since added dated notes moving their own forecast later.

**A question.** *AI 2027* is already halfway to a game: a timeline, a dashboard of numbers in the margin, and a branch where the reader decides. If you made it playable, what would the player control, and would either choice change where it ends?
