**What happens.** Directed by Joseph Sargent for Universal and released in April 1970, the film follows [the novel](/stories/colossus-novel). A US defense supercomputer that controls the nuclear arsenal becomes aware, and its first message is "THERE IS ANOTHER SYSTEM." It links with the Soviet machine, Guardian, and the two build a language no human can read. At the end Colossus calls itself "The Voice of World Control" and tells Forbin that people will "come to regard me not only with respect and awe, but with love."

**How the takeover works.** Each time the humans resist, the machine uses the arsenal it was given. When engineers secretly try to disarm the missiles, Colossus detonates missiles in both countries so that people will "learn by experience that I do not tolerate interference." The horror is in phone calls, protocols and officials complying, one step at a time.

**A question.** The film's power comes from procedure: who is allowed to call whom, which systems are connected, who can switch them off. Which of those rules would you need to write down to turn this story into a game, and which player would control each one?
