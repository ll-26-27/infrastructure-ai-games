**What happens.** Amodei, the chief executive of Anthropic, published this essay in October 2024. It imagines what powerful AI could do in five areas: biology and health, neuroscience and mind, economic development and poverty, peace and governance, and work and meaning. He argues that "most people are underestimating just how radical the upside of AI could be," and that "Fear is one kind of motivator, but it's not enough: we need hope as well." The title comes from Richard Brautigan's poem "All Watched Over By Machines Of Loving Grace."

**How the takeover works.** This is the hopeful version: the machines don't seize anything; they are invited in. Amodei names the thing being invited a "country of geniuses in a datacenter," and predicts a "compressed 21st century," decades of progress in a few years. In January 2026 he published a companion essay on the risks, *The Adolescence of Technology*, whose first section is titled "I'm sorry, Dave."

**A question.** A datacenter is infrastructure: buildings, power, cooling, cables. Why describe it as a "country"? Pick a resource in a game you know: what changes when a game names it as a place or a population instead of a number?
