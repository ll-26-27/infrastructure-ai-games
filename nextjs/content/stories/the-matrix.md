**What happens.** In the Wachowskis' film (Warner Bros., March 1999), Thomas Anderson, who goes by Neo, learns that his 1999 is a simulation the machines built to keep humans docile while they harvest their bodies' energy. He is offered a choice between a red pill and a blue pill: the truth or the comfortable world he knows.

**How the takeover works.** Totally, and in the past tense. There is no invasion to watch; the machines already run everything, and ordinary life is their interface. The only way to see the system is to step outside it.

**A question.** In *The Matrix* the infrastructure is invisible because it works. Many games are like that too: you stop noticing the rules after a few hours. What would a game have to do to make its own rules visible to you again while you are playing?
