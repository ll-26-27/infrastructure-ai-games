**What happens.** John W. Campbell's novella appeared in *Astounding Science Fiction* in August 1938, under the name Don A. Stuart. An Antarctic expedition thaws out a creature from a buried spaceship, and it can imitate any living thing it absorbs. The men realize some of them are no longer men. It was filmed as *The Thing from Another World* (1951) and as John Carpenter's *The Thing* (1982).

**How the takeover works.** By imitation from inside the group, and then by paranoia. Once anyone could be a copy, the crew can no longer trust the people they depend on. McReady's answer is a test: a heated wire touched to a blood sample makes an imitation's blood recoil. The test exposes fourteen imitations.

**A question.** The blood test is a detection mechanic: a rule that turns a hidden fact into a visible one. Games with hidden traitors need the same thing. What tests does your course's infrastructure use to sort people (a password, a badge, a form), and what does each one actually check?
