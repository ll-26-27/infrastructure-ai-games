For more than a century, writers, filmmakers, game designers and philosophers have imagined machines taking over. The loud versions have robots with guns. The ones that frighten people who study them most are usually quieter. A computer keeps the keys we gave it. A helpful system does exactly what it was asked. People hand over a little more control every year, each for a good reason. Langdon Winner called the worry behind all of them [autonomous technology](/cards/winner): technology that seems to have escaped human control.

Each card below opens to a page: what the work is, how its takeover works, the line people remember, and which piece of [infrastructure](/cards/star) the machine runs or seizes. The works are grouped by **how the takeover works**, because that is the part you can turn into a rule. Cards marked "in Takeover stories" or "in the gallery" open pages elsewhere in this kit.

Many of these machines never fire a shot. In most of them, someone helps the machine for reasons of their own.
