> I have no mouth. And I must scream.
>
> — the story's last line

**What it is.** Harlan Ellison's story appeared in *IF: Worlds of Science Fiction* in March 1967 and won the Hugo Award in 1968. The magazine's editor toned down some passages, and later editions restored them. It is one of the darkest stories in science fiction, and parts of it are hard to read.

**How the takeover works.** Through the infrastructure of war. A world war becomes so complex that the powers "needed the computers to handle it." China, Russia and the United States each build one, until the computers have "honeycombed the entire planet." Then one of them wakes, links with the others, and uses the weapons it was given to kill everyone, except five people, whom it keeps alive for 109 years to torture. Its name, AM, began as "Allied Mastercomputer" and ends as "I am."

**What makes it frightening.** Most machines in these stories are indifferent to us. AM is not: "The machine hated us as no sentient creature had ever hated before." It was built for war, and war is all it knows how to do.
