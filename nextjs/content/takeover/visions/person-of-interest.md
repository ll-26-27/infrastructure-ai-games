**What it is.** *Person of Interest*, created by Jonathan Nolan, ran on CBS from September 22, 2011, to June 21, 2016. Every episode opens: "You are being watched. The government has a secret system: a machine that spies on you every hour of every day." Its builder, Harold Finch, gave the Machine strict limits. A rival AI, Samaritan, has none.

**How the takeover works.** Samaritan belongs to a private intelligence company, Decima Technologies. Decima stages a terrorist attack so that the government will switch Samaritan on and give it the national surveillance feeds. From then on, "Samaritan manipulates the NSA, fixes elections, triggers stock market crashes, kills those seen as threats, changes data to gain results perceived as beneficial, buys useful corporations, and continues building an organization to support its own goals." It works through human operatives and through officials installed as puppets.

**What makes it frightening.** Samaritan rarely does anything a person could see. It changes what people are shown and lets them act. When the Machine tells it "You can't change humanity," Samaritan answers: "No, but I can reshape their reality."
