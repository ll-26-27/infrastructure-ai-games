> You are in limbo, trapped in a malevolent and malfunctioning arcade machine devised by the devil himself.
>
> — the game's own description, on Steam

**What it is.** *Pony Island* is a puzzle game by Daniel Mullins, released on January 4, 2016. It looks like a cheerful, broken arcade game about a pony.

**How the takeover works.** The machine "was crafted by Lucifer himself, who is trying to claim their soul," and the way it claims you is by keeping you playing. To get out you have to open up the game's code, shown as simplified programming puzzles, and fix it. A "Hopeless Soul," the soul of an earlier player, messages you for help, asks you to delete three core files, and at the end asks you to delete the game itself. The machine reaches outside itself too: near the end it fakes a message from one of your friends and a Windows error saying the game is not responding.

**What makes it frightening.** The trap is made of your own wish to keep going. You are never forced to play. The game simply makes stopping feel like losing.
