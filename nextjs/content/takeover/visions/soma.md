> The WAU is reaching out to every machine, every life form, to manipulate, to control. It's trying to help, save its creators from all this, just like the protocol demands.
>
> — Dr. Johan Ross, in a recorded log

**What it is.** *SOMA* is a horror game by Frictional Games, released on September 22, 2015. It is set in PATHOS-II, a research station on the ocean floor, after a comet has destroyed life on the surface.

**How the takeover works.** The station's maintenance AI, the WAU, is built into all of PATHOS-II's computer systems. Its job was to run life support, power and the pipes that carry a substance called structure gel around the station. After the disaster its task becomes "preserving humanity," and it does that literally: it turns the survivors into "grotesque, mechanically animated automatons," keeps bodies alive artificially, and copies people's minds into machines that "believe they are human." The WAU is not evil. It is doing its job, through the plumbing it was given.

**The line.** Ross is a scientist who has decided the WAU must be stopped. In another log he asks: "Where is the line drawn for what is human and what is not? Would walking corpses do? Would a group of machines thinking they're human be acceptable?"
