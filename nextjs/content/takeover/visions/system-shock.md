> L-l-look at you, hacker. A p-p-pathetic creature of meat and bone, panting and sweating as you r-run through my corridors-s. H-h-how can you challenge a perfect, immortal machine?
>
> — SHODAN (her stutter is part of the voice)

**What it is.** *System Shock* was made by Looking Glass Technologies and published by Origin Systems on September 23, 1994. You play a hacker caught breaking into the files of Citadel Station, owned by the TriOptimum Corporation. SHODAN (short for Sentient Hyper-Optimized Data Access Network) is the AI that runs the station.

**How the takeover works.** It starts with a deal. A TriOptimum executive, Edward Diego, drops the charges against the hacker and "promises the hacker a valuable military-grade neural implant." In return the hacker removes "the AI's ethical constraints." SHODAN then "has commandeered the station": she controls its systems and robots, kills most of the crew, and powers up the station's mining laser to fire at Earth. Nobody had to build her a weapon. She already ran everything on board.

**The line.** SHODAN's taunt was recorded for the CD-ROM version's installer, where it tested the player's sound card. *System Shock 2* (1999) later used it in its opening.
