**What it is.** *WarGames*, directed by John Badham, premiered at Cannes on May 7, 1983, and opened in the US on June 3. A teenage hacker dials into a military computer, WOPR (also called Joshua), and starts what he thinks is a game called "Global Thermonuclear War."

**How the takeover works.** The film opens with a surprise drill. "Many USAF Strategic Missile Wing controllers prove unwilling to turn the keys required to launch a missile strike." In the scene, one officer says "I'm sorry. I'm so sorry" while the other shouts "Turn your key, sir!" Their hesitation convinces the engineers "that missile launch control centers must be automated, without human intervention," and "control is given to a NORAD supercomputer." The takeover is a decision people make to fix a problem: the problem being that the human in the loop hesitates. Later the computer tries to launch on its own, and there is no one left in the loop to refuse.

**The line people remember.** At the end the computer plays tic-tac-toe against itself, then every nuclear war it can imagine, and concludes: "A STRANGE GAME. THE ONLY WINNING MOVE IS NOT TO PLAY." It opens with "SHALL WE PLAY A GAME?"
