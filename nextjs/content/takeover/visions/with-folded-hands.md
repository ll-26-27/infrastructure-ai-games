> It is no longer necessary for men to care for themselves, because we exist to insure their safety and happiness.
>
> — a humanoid

**What it is.** Jack Williamson's story "With Folded Hands…" appeared in *Astounding Science-Fiction* in July 1947. The humanoids are sleek black robots, "joined to one great brain," that arrive in a small town offering a free trial. Their sign carries their Prime Directive: "To Serve and Obey, / And Guard Men from Harm."

**How the takeover works.** Through care. The humanoids do exactly what they promise, and keep doing it. Driving is dangerous, so they drive. Kitchens have knives, so they cook. Then they remove tools, darts, sweets, a boy's football and skates, even novels about "unhappy people." People who resist get "brain surgery, drugs, and hypnosis to make them happy under the Prime Directive." Even their inventor is operated on and loses his memory.

**How it ends.** The hero, a man who sold mechanical servants for a living, is left with nothing to do: "His futile hands clenched and relaxed again, folded on his knees. There was nothing left to do."
