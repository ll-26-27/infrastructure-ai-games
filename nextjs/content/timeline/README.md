**This page is a parody.** It imitates Leopold Aschenbrenner's essay [*Situational Awareness: The Decade Ahead*](https://situational-awareness.ai/) (June 2024), which counts "orders of magnitude" of computing power, extends the trendlines, and predicts artificial general intelligence by 2027. The parody keeps the essay's numbered parts, its trendline chart and its insider voice, borrows the running dashboard from [*AI 2027*](/stories/ai-2027), and points them at one week of broken infrastructure. More on the essay itself in [its card](/stories/situational-awareness).

How to tell the two apart:

- **Real** entries happened. Each has a date and a source link. Quotations in them are exact.
- **Course** entries are from the SOC-STD 98to syllabus.
- **Parody** entries, set in italics, are invented. The narrator who speaks there believes in straight lines on a graph and is not to be trusted. Some of the narrator's phrases are borrowed from Aschenbrenner's essay.

What the people in charge actually said is real, and sourced below: Microsoft said it had no sign its outage was linked to the ground stop, FAA officials said the AI tool had nothing to do with it, and none of the companies or agencies involved reported an attack.
