Attackers moved about $6.3 billion in cryptocurrency out of Binance's hot wallets across ten blockchains, most of it in about twenty minutes: by the FBI's account, the largest crypto theft on record. About $3 billion was frozen on-chain. Binance said it had covered customers' losses by November 12. Just before the window of this page, but it sets the pattern of the month: a break-in through the tools engineers use every day. One headline put it down to employees "vibe coding". When a system fails, the blame tends to land on the person nearest to it: what Madeleine Clare Elish calls a moral crumple zone.
