After network problems on November 8, Monadnock Community Hospital lost its phones and other systems. Ambulances were diverted to other hospitals, elective surgeries and outpatient lab and imaging appointments were cancelled through November 30, and the date for getting back to normal kept slipping. The emergency room stayed open for walk-ins.
