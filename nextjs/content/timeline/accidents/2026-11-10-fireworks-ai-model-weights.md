Fireworks AI, which runs and fine-tunes AI models for other companies, disclosed that attackers had broken into a third-party cloud provider and used it to reach Fireworks' development machines (on October 23–24). They got billing details and incomplete copies of some customers' model weights and datasets. An earlier incident in October had exposed API keys.
