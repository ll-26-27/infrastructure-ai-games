Ransomware hit the Russian host ESTT (November 8) and the Belarusian cloud provider ActiveCloud (disclosed November 10), taking down customers' websites, email and virtual servers across Belarus until about November 13. On November 17 the Russian host Timeweb Cloud had a large outage of its own, with more than 5,000 complaints in a day. These followed a data-wiping attack on another Russian host, Beget, on November 2.
