Anthropic's Claude had two outages on November 13: sign-in failures, then a broader outage of the apps, the API and Claude Code. Scheduled tasks ran more than two hours late and some runs were skipped. Separately, from November 12 to 13, Claude's and ChatGPT's connections to Microsoft 365 failed because Microsoft itself was down. ChatGPT had a short login incident on November 11, and Claude a short one on the morning of the workshop.
