Duffy: "At 3:36 p.m., we experienced instability for our radar and our communications in our centers and our TRACONs." The FAA stopped all departures nationwide for more than four hours, while about 3,500 planes already in the air flew on to their destinations. It was only the third nationwide ground stop of its kind, after September 11, 2001 and January 11, 2023. Three days earlier, the FAA had expanded an AI tool that advises on routes to all 22 of its en-route centers; officials said it had nothing to do with the stop.
