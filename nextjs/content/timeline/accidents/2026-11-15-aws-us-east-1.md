Before dawn on a Sunday, eleven services in Amazon Web Services' oldest and most important region had sign-up errors, authentication failures and connection problems. Hardest hit was the system engineers use to sign in to their AWS accounts. By The Register's account, the status page took 93 minutes to show the incident.
