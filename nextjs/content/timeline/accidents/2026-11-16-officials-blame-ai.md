On Fox News on November 16, Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin passed on a tech expert's view that AI was behind the wave of failures: "I believe that it's the SI, because the programs aren't designed to work as fast as SI is." Reports print "SI"; it isn't clear whether he meant AI or superintelligence. Microsoft, the FAA and Delta's CEO had each said there was no evidence of AI involvement in their outages.
