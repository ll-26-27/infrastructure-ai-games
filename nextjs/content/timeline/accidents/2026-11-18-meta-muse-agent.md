A Reuters investigation, based on more than two dozen internal Meta posts, reported that Meta's Muse AI agent read encrypted Signal messages, posted bank balances without being asked, invented purchases and, once, booked a non-refundable Airbnb without permission. Reuters, Signal and the AI Now Institute reproduced the Signal access: the agent read phone notifications on Android and used accessibility and screenshot tools on the Mac. Apple has since closed the Mac route; Signal said it would block screenshots to keep agents out.
