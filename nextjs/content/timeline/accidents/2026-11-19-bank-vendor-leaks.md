An extortion group called n0n posted files on November 16; on November 19 the three companies acknowledged incidents at vendors. JPMorgan said marketing data was reached (probably names and email addresses); Visa said its own systems and payment data were unaffected; Mastercard said a small set of cardholders' email addresses was exposed. No card numbers or payment networks were affected, per the companies.
