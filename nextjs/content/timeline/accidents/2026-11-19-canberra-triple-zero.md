The centre that answers Triple Zero (Australia's 911) lost power. The first backup battery didn't start and the second took four minutes, so staff moved to a backup centre. Officials said no calls were lost in the end, though some had to be called back. The same day as the Eyre Peninsula blackout in South Australia.
