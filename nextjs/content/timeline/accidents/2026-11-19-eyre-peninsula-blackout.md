Three towers on the single 270-km transmission line to the Eyre Peninsula fell in a storm, cutting power to about 20,000–24,000 properties. Businesses and schools closed. The line's operator says the towers will be back "in a few months' time". A second circuit that could have provided backup had been considered when the line was built (opened 2023) and rejected because it would have doubled the cost.
